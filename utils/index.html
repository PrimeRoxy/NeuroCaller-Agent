Features

Everything a calling team needs, in one place

Live call monitor

Every call streams into the dashboard the moment it starts, with its status, lead signal, and the reason behind it.

Launch from a chat

Copilot reads your numbers, scripts, and FAQs from text or files and runs the campaign.

Post-call analysis

Each call is classified as qualified, follow-up, not interested, or unreachable, with the next best step.

Callbacks on time

When a lead says "call me tomorrow at 4", the callback is scheduled for then, outside your quiet hours.

Recordings and transcripts

Play any call back and read who said what, turn by turn.

Campaign controls and CRM sync

Pause, resume, or cancel any campaign mid-flight. Outcomes and follow-ups sync to Salesforce so your reps see them where they already work.

FAQ

Questions teams ask first

Which phone networks does NeuroCaller use?

Calls go out through VoiceLink, Plivo, or Twilio depending on the number and country you call from. You choose the numbers in Agent studio.

What languages and accents are available?

Indian voices for Hindi and English conversations, plus a set of global English voices. You can preview every voice on this page and in Agent studio.

Are calls recorded?

Yes. Each call keeps a recording and a transcript, and gets an analysis with the outcome, sales stage, customer tone, and recommended next step.

How are follow-ups handled?

If a lead asks to be called back at a certain time, NeuroCaller schedules that callback automatically. Calls never go out during your quiet hours.

Can I stop a campaign once it starts?

Yes. Pause, resume, or cancel any campaign from the Campaigns screen. Calls already in progress finish normally.

Does it work with my CRM?

Salesforce is supported today. Add your connected-app credentials in Agent studio and outcomes sync to your leads.
